About this guide

This app ranks Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Freedom Flex and Freedom Unlimited, and tracks selected Chase credits locally on your device.

Benefit definitions are configuration-driven in app.js. No Chase login, account data or card numbers are stored or transmitted.
