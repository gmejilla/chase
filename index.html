About this guide

This app ranks your Chase Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Freedom Flex, and Freedom Unlimited using the earning rates and Q4 2026 rotating categories built into this version.

For stores, the app uses a local merchant-to-category lookup. If a store is not recognized, search the purchase category instead.

No account data is stored or transmitted.
